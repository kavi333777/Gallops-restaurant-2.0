WELCOME TO GALLOPS

Where timeless charm
meets exceptional dining.

Nestled inside the Mahalaxmi Racecourse, Gallops Restaurant offers an elegant fine-dining experience surrounded by nostalgic charm, beautiful interiors and views of the racecourse.

OUR STORY

A Mumbai dining classic.

Gallops Restaurant is a fine-dining destination located inside the Royal Western India Turf Club at Mahalaxmi.

With its charming and nostalgic ambience, Gallops brings together Indian and international flavours in an elegant setting.

Guests can enjoy both indoor dining and outdoor seating overlooking the Mahalaxmi Racecourse.
